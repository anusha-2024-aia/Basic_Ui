USER PROBLEM

The real problem isn't only the emergency.

Safety decisions happen before a woman ever reaches the point where she needs an SOS button.

6:00 PM Bus stop is crowded.

Same location. Lower perceived risk.

9:00 PM Same bus stop is empty.

Same location. Completely different situation.

01

Isolation

A person may feel unsafe while waiting alone, walking through an isolated street or travelling late at night.

02

No civic reporting loop

Dead streetlights and unsafe locations may be reported individually, but repeated community reports need a visible action trail.

03

Phone-call dependence

Calling family or friends may not be possible during a threatening situation, especially when drawing attention could make things worse.

04

Static safety information

A fixed safety score cannot represent how a place changes from evening to late night.

DESIGN QUESTION
“How might we help a woman make a safer decision before the situation becomes an emergency?”
